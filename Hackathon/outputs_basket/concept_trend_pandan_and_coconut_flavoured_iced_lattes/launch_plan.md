## Timing

In the launch wards, tea & coffee is strongest over **Jan, Feb, Mar** (+16% vs their yearly average). A launch in **Dec** puts the product on shelf as that season starts (rule of thumb).
Based on one year (2015), so a one-off event could look like a season.

## How much to trust this

- **Hidden-category test:** with tea & coffee removed from matching, the twins were still closer to the anchor on it than 84% of random sets of wards (p = 0.155).
- **Stability:** dropping one feature at a time kept on average 97% of the top-10 list (lowest 70%, when eggs was dropped).
- **Limits:** 2015 data; Tesco Clubcard shoppers only; store coverage is uneven across London; areas are described by their typical item, not individual shoppers; 'sells well in' is a place, not sales figures. This suggests where a trial is most likely to fit, not guaranteed sales.
